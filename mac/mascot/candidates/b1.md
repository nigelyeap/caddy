Idea: Caddiy is a soft light-teal blob who lives inside an amber scan ring, with an amber probe-tip dot on its head.
Why kids like it: it looks squishy and calm, like a pet you could hold, and the big round eyes are easy to love.
Draw it (6 shapes): 1. big amber circle ring. 2. wide rounded blob over the bottom of the ring. 3 and 4. two round eyes. 5. one smile arc. 6. small amber dot on top.
CAD and 3D hint: the ring is the scan sweeping around an object, and the dot is the probe tip touching it.
Risk: a round face with a top dot can read as a baby or a rattle, and the blob has no arms, so pointing must come from the dot or a squish.

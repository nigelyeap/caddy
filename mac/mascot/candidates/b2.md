Idea: Caddy is a little cube-shaped robot cat with a white rounded-square body and two big amber triangle ears.
Why kids like it: it is a pet and a robot at once, and the pointy ears make it look alert and ready to help.
Draw it (6 shapes): 1 and 2. two amber triangle ears. 3. one big rounded square over them. 4 and 5. two round eyes. 6. one smile arc.
CAD and 3D hint: the body is a cube, the first shape you make in CAD, and the ears are simple triangle primitives.
Risk: no tail fits in 6 shapes, and a white square cat face sits close to many existing cat icons, so it needs its amber ears to stand out.

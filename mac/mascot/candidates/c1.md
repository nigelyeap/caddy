Idea: Caddiy is a magnifier whose amber lens ring is the scan ring, with a happy face inside the glass.
Why kids like it: it is a detective tool that looks closely at things, and the round face is easy to love.
Draw it (5 shapes): 1 big circle with a thick amber ring, 2 rounded stick for the handle at the bottom right, 3 and 4 two dot eyes, 5 one smile arc.
CAD hint: the ring is the same amber scan ring the probe shows, so Caddiy "sees" your object to turn it into 3D.
Risk: a magnifier is a common search icon, so it may read as "search" before it reads as a character.

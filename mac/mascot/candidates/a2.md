Idea: Caddy is a soft rounded box with an amber scan ring floating over its head, the same ring the probe uses to scan.
Why kids like it: a chunky squishy box with big eyes feels like a pet, and the glowing ring can spin when Caddy is scanning or thinking.
Draw it (5 shapes): 1 a rounded square, 2 a flat oval ring above it, 3 and 4 two big dot eyes, 5 one curved smile.
CAD hint: the ring is the scan ring from the app, and it can sweep down the box in the tutorial to show scanning.
Risk: the ring can read as an angel halo, and the front-on box is less obviously 3D than A1.

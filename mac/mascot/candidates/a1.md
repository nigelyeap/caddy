Idea: Caddiy is a plain 3D cube with a happy face on the front, the first block every CAD model starts from.
Why kids like it: it looks like a toy block or a sugar cube that smiles back, and its flat front face is a big blank screen for expressions.
Draw it (6 shapes): 1 a square, 2 a slanted top on the square, 3 a slanted side on the right, 4 and 5 two big dot eyes, 6 one curved smile.
CAD hint: it is drawn like a CAD view of a box, with three visible faces in three teal shades.
Risk: very generic, other cube faces exist, and at 16 px the smile goes thin so the eyes carry the face.

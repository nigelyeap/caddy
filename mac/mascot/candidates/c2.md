Idea: Caddy is a chunky amber ruler with white tick marks down one side and a face on the other.
Why kids like it: every kid owns a ruler, and a ruler that smiles and waves feels like a school friend.
Draw it (5 shapes): 1 tall rounded rectangle, tilted a little, 2 a row of short white tick lines down the left edge, 3 and 4 two dot eyes, 5 one smile arc.
CAD hint: rulers measure size, and changing sizes ("make it 2 cm taller") is what kids do to their 3D model.
Risk: at 16 px the face and ticks blur into an amber block, and the ruler feels 2D more than 3D.

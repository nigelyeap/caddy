# Caddiy round 2: soft and round
- soft1: pale pillow cube with a carry handle and a soft seam for the cube top (3D). soft2: teal dumpling with a bag-charm carry loop. soft2 is the pick, so the three expressions use its body.
- Fixed from round 1: big eyes (about 20 percent of face width) set low and wide, each with a white sparkle. Wide U or open smile close under the eyes. Blush cheeks. No black outline, only a soft darker teal one.
- Each file is 8 flat shapes: body, 2 eyes, 2 sparkles, 2 cheeks, 1 mouth. The handle, loop, seam and pointing arm are part of the body outline.
- Draw soft2: 1) a wide squishy dumpling with a little loop on top. 2) two big dots low on the face. 3) a white dot in the top outer corner of each. 4) a half-circle smile between them. 5) two pink circles beside the smile.
- Checked at 512, 32 and 16 px. Both still show eyes, sparkles, smile and cheeks at 32 px. At 16 px the sparkles fade but the face holds.
- Risks: soft1 can read as a lunchbox or a pot with a lid. soft2 has no 3D cue because the 8-shape limit leaves no room. Adding a tiny cube in the loop would make it 9 shapes.
- Risks: the pointing arm reads as a stub, not a hand. The thinking pose only hints at the look up and right, since the eyes have no whites.
